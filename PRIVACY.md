# Typewall privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Typewall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

- Your block list, time rules, custom passages, and unlock state are stored on your own device with `chrome.storage`. They never leave your browser.
- Typewall checks the address of each page you open against your block list. This check runs locally. Addresses are not logged or sent anywhere.
- Typewall makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no remote servers.

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/iBala/typewall/issues.
